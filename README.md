Turn any photo and caption into GTA VI-style cover art: a four-panel poster
(one big hero photo left, three strips right) over an orange-to-pink band with
a bold title, a script subtitle, a gold VI badge and palm silhouettes.

Add your own photos, drag each panel to crop, pick a layout and a colour
grade, edit the title, subtitle and badge, then export a 1280 × 720 PNG.
Everything runs offline in the browser — photos never leave your machine.

Built for the [make.fun gta6-cover-art-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).
